Find your CTE pathway

Career and Technical Education (CTE) pathways are sets of three classes that build real job skills. Pick one that matches what you like, and take its classes in order during high school.

  1. Level 1 An intro class to try out the subject.
  2. Level 2 Build on it with more hands-on skills.
  3. Level 3 Finish the pathway and become a completer.
